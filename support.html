BMBI Fitness app support

BMBI Fitness helps members view classes, manage bookings, and track remaining sessions.

For sign-in, invitation, booking, or package questions, email bmbifitness@gmail.com. Include the email address on your account and a short description of the problem. Do not email your password.

To delete your account, sign in and open Account → Delete my account. The app asks you to confirm with your current password. If you cannot sign in, email us for help.
