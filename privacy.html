BMBI Fitness app privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Francesco Cataldi operates the BMBI Fitness app. Contact bmbifitness@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information used by the app

The app stores your name, email address or phone number, optional home address, account password in hashed form, class reservations and attendance, and package session balances. It uses this information to provide sign-in, invitations, password recovery, scheduling, and gym account management. The app does not ask for medical records or payment-card details in its scheduling-only release.

Sharing and security

Your account and booking information is available to you and the gym owner. The service currently stores account data on the gym’s Mac Mini, uses Tailscale to make the client service reachable, and uses an email provider to send invitations and verification messages. It does not use advertising trackers or sell account information.

Keeping and deleting information

Information remains in the active service while your account is open. To delete your account and linked app records, sign in and use Account → Delete my account. You can also email us for help with an account you cannot access. Backup copies, if maintained, may remain until their scheduled expiration. Some records may need to be retained if required by law.

For help, see app support.
